# Dr. C. Sivananda Reddy
Educator | Researcher | Techno-Cognitive Thinker

I am an educator, researcher and embedded systems engineer with a deep interest in understanding intelligence, engineering intelligent systems and critically examining the relationship between humans and technology.

My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of Brain–Computer Interfaces (BCI), Embedded and Edge Artificial Intelligence, Intelligent Hardware Architectures and Cognitive Studies.

I am currently a faculty member at Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), India.

I hold a Ph.D. from Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), where my doctoral research focused on Brain Computer Interfaces.

Beyond engineering, I am fascinated by fundamental questions concerning the human brain, cognition, intelligence and the growing influence of technology on human thought and behaviour.

My intellectual journey is driven by three interconnected pursuits:

Understanding Intelligence. Engineering Intelligent Systems. Questioning Technology.

## My Academic Journey

My academic journey has been shaped by a continuing interest in electronics, embedded computing, artificial intelligence and engineering education.

With over 13 years of teaching experience, I have been involved in undergraduate engineering education, practical laboratory instruction, technical mentoring and academic activities.

My doctoral research at IIT Madras strengthened my interest in resource-efficient artificial intelligence and its implementation on embedded computing platforms.

Over time, my interests have expanded from conventional electronic systems towards neural intelligence, Brain–Computer Interfaces, intelligent hardware architectures and interdisciplinary questions about cognition and technology.

I see these areas not as isolated disciplines but as interconnected approaches to understanding and engineering intelligence.

## My Research Interests

My research interests span four interconnected domains.

### 01. Neural Intelligence and Brain–Computer Interfaces

I am interested in understanding how information is represented and processed in the human brain and how neural activity can be interpreted using computational techniques.

My interests include EEG signal processing, Brain–Computer Interfaces, neural network architectures, deep learning for neural signals and brain-inspired computing.

### 02. Embedded and Edge Intelligence

I investigate how artificial intelligence can be implemented efficiently on resource-constrained computing platforms.

My interests include TinyML, embedded deep learning, model quantisation, neural network optimisation, low-power Edge AI and deployment on microcontrollers and embedded processors.

### 03. Intelligent Hardware and Computing Architectures

I explore the design and implementation of computing architectures that enable efficient artificial intelligence.

My interests include FPGA-based AI acceleration, VLSI architectures, System-on-Chip design, hardware–software co-design and energy-efficient neural network implementations.

### 04. Techno-Cognitive Studies and Critical Technology Inquiry

My intellectual interests extend beyond engineering into questions concerning human cognition, technological dependence, artificial intelligence and human agency.

I am particularly interested in examining how technology shapes human thinking, learning, decision-making and society.

I believe technological progress should be accompanied by critical reflection on its necessity, consequences and influence on human life.

## My Teaching Philosophy

Teaching has been an important part of my professional journey.

I believe engineering education should not be limited to delivering information, preparing students for examinations or training them to operate existing technologies.

It should cultivate curiosity, conceptual understanding, practical competence, independent thinking and the confidence to solve unfamiliar problems.

I strive to connect theoretical concepts with practical experimentation, hardware implementation and real-world engineering challenges.

I am particularly committed to making high-quality technical education accessible and meaningful to students from diverse socio-economic backgrounds, including rural and middle-class communities.

My aspiration is to help students develop the knowledge, confidence and competencies necessary to build meaningful careers and contribute to technological advancement.

## Technical Exploration and Projects

My technical activities involve investigating intelligent algorithms and implementing them on practical computing platforms.

Some of the areas I have explored include:

- EEG-based Brain–Computer Interface systems and neural signal classification.

- Deep learning models for brain signal analysis.

- TinyML and Edge AI on STM32 microcontrollers.

- PyTorch-based model development, ONNX conversion and quantisation.

- Resource-efficient neural network deployment.

- FPGA-based artificial intelligence acceleration.

- Hardware–software integration for intelligent embedded systems.

I value reproducible experimentation, systematic performance evaluation and the practical understanding of how intelligent algorithms behave under real hardware constraints.

## Beyond Engineering: Questioning Technology

My interests are not confined to developing increasingly sophisticated technological systems.

I am equally interested in understanding what technological advancement means for human intelligence, autonomy, creativity and society.

The ability to create a technology does not automatically establish its necessity or value.

I believe engineers and researchers must ask not only whether something is technically possible, but also whether it is meaningful, responsible and beneficial.

This perspective motivates my broader identity as a Techno-Cognitive Thinker.

A question that guides my thinking is:

**Just because we can, should we?**
